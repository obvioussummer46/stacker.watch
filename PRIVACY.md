# Stacker Watch privacy policy

Effective 29 September 2026.

Stacker Watch is a read-only Apple Watch client for [stacker.news](https://stacker.news),
published by Bittel. It is an independent project and is not affiliated with stacker.news.

## What the app collects

Nothing. Stacker Watch has no accounts, no login, no analytics, no advertising and no
crash reporting of its own. It does not collect, store or share any personal data.

## Network requests

To show posts the app sends anonymous queries to the public stacker.news API at
`https://stacker.news/api/graphql`. Like any web request, this exposes your device's IP
address to stacker.news. Those requests are governed by the
[stacker.news privacy policy](https://stacker.news/privacy). The app sends no
identifiers, credentials or personal information with these requests.

## Data stored on your watch

Feeds, posts and the territory list are cached on the watch so that content loads
instantly and can be read offline. Your screen configuration and text size setting are
also stored on the watch. All of this stays on the device and is removed when you delete
the app.

## Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs and shares no data with anyone.
